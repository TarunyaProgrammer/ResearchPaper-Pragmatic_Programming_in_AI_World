[Raw Commit Data / Logs] ──> [Filtering & Sanitization] ──> [Feature Extraction] ──> [Statistical Analysis]

## Threats to Validity
1. **Construct Validity**: Ensuring proxy metrics (e.g., refactoring commit frequency) accurately reflect architectural technical debt.
2. **Internal Validity**: Controlling for developer experience levels and domain complexity.
3. **External Validity**: Generalizability across different programming languages and LLM model architectures.
